## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is hiring teams reviewing Aditya Gollapudi's portfolio. They need to understand the quality of his design thinking, research judgment, testing process, and ability to carry an individual project from discovery through validation.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Aditya's industrial-design and UX/UI work. The Forkast and Cura usability-testing reports show how he independently planned, conducted, interpreted, and applied prototype testing—not to reproduce academic assignments.

## Positioning

Forkast is a two-sided restaurant-allergy safety system connecting the diner-facing decision with the restaurant workflow needed to verify and execute it. Aditya completed the project independently, including talking with participants, research, interface design, prototyping, usability sessions, analysis, and iteration.

Cura is a medication-routine concept focused on setup, daily dose status, late-dose recovery, and bounded caregiver support. Its testing report follows two moderated remote rounds with four different participants in each round.

## Operating Context

Forkast serves four roles across customer and restaurant contexts: diner, waiter, chef, and line cook. Customer-side testing involved 11 participants. A separate restaurant-side test of the first iteration involved three people representing a waiter, chef, and junior chef; this test led to the redesign described in the full-process case study's “uncomfortable conversation” section.
